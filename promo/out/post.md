- A Pomodoro timer that grows a living pond
- Focus for 25 minutes. Watch the pond change.
- One HTML file, zero dependencies, a tiny ecosystem

I built a small focus timer for people who like seeing progress instead of collecting streak badges.

Eco-Pomodoro is an eco-friendly Pomodoro timer: every completed focus session advances a tiny pond. Plankton multiplies, weeds grow, fish breed and pass on their color, tadpoles become frogs, and koi unlock after 20 hours of real focus time. Breaks slow the ecology down; idle time freezes it.

It is a single index.html with no build step, no account and no network requests. Saves stay in localStorage and can be exported as JSON. Timing uses timestamps rather than frame counts, so switching tabs or refreshing does not drift the countdown.

Try the browser demo: https://timyin7.github.io/Eco-Pomodoro/
Source and setup notes: https://github.com/timyin7/Eco-Pomodoro
